# Opening Hours and Appointments

Enamel Dental is open Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 18:00 and Saturday from
9:00 to 14:00. The clinic is closed on Sundays and on public holidays.

Appointments can be booked by phone, through the patient portal, or with the
clinic assistant. Please arrive ten minutes before your appointment so the front
desk can complete your check-in.

## Late arrivals

If you arrive more than fifteen minutes late we may need to reschedule your
visit so that other patients are not delayed. There is no charge for
rescheduling a late arrival.

## Walk-in visits

We keep a small number of same-day slots for urgent dental pain. Call the clinic
first to check whether a walk-in slot is available.
