# Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy

We ask for at least 24 hours notice if you need to cancel or move an
appointment. This gives us time to offer the slot to another patient who is
waiting for care.

## Cancelling

You can cancel through the patient portal, by phone, or by asking the clinic
assistant. Cancelling through the assistant requires you to confirm your
identity and the appointment you want to cancel.

## Rescheduling

A reschedule moves your existing appointment to a new time. If the new time is
not available we will offer the nearest alternatives. Repeated no-shows may
affect your ability to book online.

## Missed appointments

A missed appointment without notice is recorded as a no-show. After two
no-shows in a rolling twelve month period we may ask you to confirm future
appointments by phone.

## Emergencies

Cancellation notice does not apply to a genuine dental emergency. Call the
clinic directly and we will make room for you as soon as possible.
