# Insurance and Payment FAQ

## Which insurance plans do you accept?

We accept most major dental insurance plans. The front desk can confirm whether
your specific plan is accepted before your appointment. Please bring your
insurance card and a photo ID to your first visit.

## Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. For treatments over a set threshold the clinic can arrange an interest-free
instalment plan. Ask the reception team for the current terms and the documents
you need to provide.

## How much will my treatment cost?

Fees depend on the treatment and on your insurance coverage. Reception can give
you a written estimate before treatment begins so there are no surprises.

## Can I pay by card?

We accept debit cards, major credit cards and bank transfer. We do not accept
cash for treatments above the clinic cash limit.

## Do you offer a discount for families?

Families booking two or more appointments in the same week may be eligible for a
sibling discount. The front desk can apply it at booking time.
